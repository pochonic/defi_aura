# Crypto Radar Platform Roadmap

## Phase 1: Solana LP intelligence

Raydium, Orca and Meteora LP opportunity data, explainable scoring, risk modules and persisted history.

## Phase 2: Solana Lending

Connect lending data sources and expose supply, borrow, utilization and liquidity intelligence.

## Phase 3: Stablecoin Intelligence

Add stablecoin yield, liquidity, asset-risk and depeg-risk analysis.

## Phase 4: Multi-chain

Enable Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum after their providers and data quality are validated.

## Phase 5: Portfolio + Alerts

Only after the intelligence layers are stable: portfolio tracking, alerts and related user features.
